# Rollover contact and disturbed ground

This pass addresses long side/roof sliding and the absence of visible ground disturbance after a rollover. The final Windows package passes 2,306 native checks and 230 new portable checks, with 342 fresh captures actually reviewed. [validation-native.md](validation-native.md) binds those results to the exact executable and fresh full cook. Earlier rejected candidates and diagnostic control runs are excluded from acceptance.

The upright undertray retains its low sliding/static friction so ordinary crests and terrain transitions do not become sudden brakes. As the body rolls away from its supporting surface normal, a smooth transition applies ground-specific panel friction. Snow retains more sliding than dry pavement; rain reduces the authored grip values. A vehicle correctly supported by its tires inside the loop uses the constructed track normal, preserving normal inverted driving. The coefficients are simulation tuning, rather than measured manufacturer coating specifications.

Unreal's live Chaos material is updated explicitly, including welded body shapes. Chaos resolves the actual collision friction, angular response and settling. The change does not zero the vehicle's velocity, add drag in the air, or automatically brake a vehicle that lands back on supporting tires.

Actual terrain-body collision notifications provide contact positions and normal impulses. Those events are queued until the game tick, avoiding terrain mutation inside a physics callback. Duplicate nearby welded contacts are merged; at most four strong support patches work the soil per tick. Broad panel patches use actual supported load, finite available soil depth and bounded compaction, with displaced material returned to low shoulders. Body impressions carry no tire-tread direction, including after soil-cache eviction. Grass plants within the contact patch are flattened through the existing vegetation system.

Hard dirt receives a shallow scuff; mud, loose soil and snow receive physical impressions. Pavement and rock receive pale, broken scrape fragments without excavating the rigid ground. A dedicated project-owned vertex-color material keeps these marks distinct from tire rubber. Ground disturbance remains active in Free Run when vehicle damage is disabled.

The body-mark history is limited to 1,024 patches. Geometry is divided into sections of 32 patches, and only affected sections are rebuilt. Existing scuffs follow later ground deformation. Recovery preserves the disturbance; resetting the terrain clears it. These are session-local marks and bounded height-field deformation, without persistent map saving or volumetric soil simulation.

`Tools/body-ground-material.py` creates the project-owned material. The regular asset importer invokes it for a clean rebuild; a change to that material requires a fresh cook. No acquired Epic/Fab source assets or vehicle meshes are modified by this pass.

`Tools/verify-body-ground.ps1` runs 39 native trials: all nine vehicles coasting on left/right sides and roofs on pavement, plus stock-Wrangler side/roof drops on six other surfaces. Each moving trial seeds approximately 18 mph once and then observes the actual physics. It checks settling, legitimate tire-supported recovery, clearance, disabled overturned drive, real body contact, rendered scuffs, physical soft-ground compaction and rigid no-excavation. The six-case quick variant covers Wrangler, Sprinter and G580. Its explicit legacy control restores the old low-friction setting only in automated validation, providing a same-executable comparison. Controls and earlier rejected captures do not contribute to final acceptance totals.

`Tools/test-body-ground.cmd` checks the shared grip transition and impression-depth calculation. Native regressions also cover rollover support, momentum/suspension, wheel motion, challenge routes, ruts and visible damage. Test runs isolate player files, request a 20 FPS cap and enforce a sampled 77°C GPU cutoff. The previously measured quarry CPU-physics bottleneck remains unresolved; these contact changes do not establish a frame-rate or PC-crash fix.
