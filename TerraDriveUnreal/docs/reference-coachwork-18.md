# Vehicle reference craft — pass 18

This pass refines all nine existing vehicle models. It concentrates on the depth and construction of the lights, visible panel joints, cabin forms and wheel finishes. The stock dimensions, axle centres, mechanical tire sizes, upgrade mounting points and native C++ simulation remain unchanged. The browser implementation remains preserved.

Fresh native import and full cooking succeeded. All **2,905 native checks** pass and **450 fresh captures were actually reviewed**, including ten close views per vehicle. Nine source validators and 24 new physical detail probes pass. The [exact validation record](validation-native.md) binds the final assets, views and limitations; source geometry checks alone do not establish visual accuracy.

The circular G-Class lens diameter was checked against the archived manufacturer front drawing and retained. Its former small round central pupil and missing larger rectangular black housing made the face read incorrectly; enlarging the complete lens would have contradicted the dimensioned drawing. Factory panel locations and reconstructed local curvatures are different kinds of evidence: the latter remain estimates.

Actual inspection of the first cooked candidate's 90 views found a mismatched Urus front closure: the fascia had been raised around the lamp opening while the adjoining shoulder retained its earlier boundary. The outer lamp/fascia edge stood above the bonnet in the overhead close-up. That candidate was rejected despite passing its 99 numerical render checks. A subsequent full-edge source diagnostic found a roughly 0.85-inch interior gap even after the corners matched; the strip's endpoint varied with its interpolation coordinate. Fixed transverse endpoints close the shoulder edge. A later preview still had a narrow front crown/fascia slit: smooth crown interpolation rose above the actual polygonal fascia boundary, missing 60 of 73 actual face-ray probes. Only two of that preview's 90 images were inspected before rejection. The replacement uses one shared piecewise-linear top boundary, includes every crest station in the crown grid, and fits the lamp opening, clear cover and Y guide inside the surrounding sheet. Six new boundary probes inspect both complete shoulder edges, both front seams and both painted lamp rims. Rejected checks and uninspected images are excluded from final acceptance totals.

The previous machined wheel-face strips stood about 0.94 inches ahead of the casting. They now sit about 0.07 inches above it, with a thin closed section. The new grille tooling produces real open cells and inward-facing walls rather than a dark texture pasted onto an intact face.

## Import cost and verification

Material IDs are retained by their complete exported surface signature. A new seat fabric therefore does not renumber every later scenery material and OBJ. The current change set has nine body meshes and six Wrangler/Bronco/HUMMER rim or combined-wheel meshes, plus the import manifest. The other 259 source assets, including the scenery, are byte-identical to the checkpoint. No new acquired asset or reference-photo dependency is introduced.

The source validators preserve the existing geometric limits. An initial G 63 candidate exceeded the existing 450,000-triangle body/cabin budget; new lamp/mirror tessellation was reduced and the original limit was retained. The 24 new physical probes inspect actual Urus optical depth, visible lens layers, absence of painted chamber fill, the complete shared shoulder/front edges and continuous painted lamp rims; G-Class housing dimensions, rear-facing mirror normals/visibility, open radiator cells and the original triangle budget. Existing nine-vehicle checks still cover normals, genuine glazing, wheel/rim/tire fit, body envelopes, straight Civic pillars, bonnet/deck relief and variant-specific geometry.

This is still original procedural reference geometry. Exact lamp tooling, complete production cabins, baked detail maps, authored LODs and manufacturer-CAD accuracy remain outside its demonstrated quality. The [vehicle art production plan](vehicle-art-production.md) explains the remaining path to a Forza-class asset.
