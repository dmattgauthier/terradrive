# Mercedes G 580 and AMG G 63: pass 14 research and original geometry

Research checked October 27, 2026. The target is the **2025 modern G 580 with EQ Technology** and **2025 Mercedes-AMG G 63**, rather than the older W463, a Brabus conversion, a G 63 4×4² or the earlier Concept EQG. This document describes source work and integration recommendations. It is not native-game acceptance, an OEM CAD reconstruction or a claim of Forza-level fidelity.

The original model authoring module is [mercedes-gclass.mjs](../Tools/mercedes-gclass.mjs). Reference photographs, downloaded research pages and private source checks belong under `Saved/Expedition14References/GClass`; they are inspection material and are excluded from public asset publication. The body uses authored vertices, curves and primitives. No photograph is a vehicle texture and no external vehicle mesh is included.

## Verified specification choices

Both vehicles use a **113.8-inch wheelbase**. The recommended common US stock tire fitment is **275/50R20**, with a calculated nominal radius of **15.41 inches**, width **10.83 inches** and diameter **30.83 inches**. Tire dimensions are nominal sidewall arithmetic, not measured loaded radii. The electric model has four independently controlled motors and a two-speed transmission per motor; the motors are mounted to the frame, rather than being hub motors. Its rear suspension is De-Dion, while the G 63 has a driven solid rear axle. Both have independent double-wishbone front suspension. [Mercedes-Benz USA 2025 G 580 guide](https://media.mbusa.com/news/2025-mercedes-benz-g-580-with-eq-technology-quick-reference-guide), [2025 G 63 guide](https://media.mbusa.com/news/2025-mercedes-amg-g-63-quick-reference-guide), [electric launch technical data](https://media.mbusa.com/news/the-gelandewagen-electrified).

| --- | --- | --- |
| Rated propulsion | 579 hp / 432 kW; 859 lb-ft / 1,164 Nm aggregate | 577 hp / 430 kW at 6,000 rpm; 627 lb-ft / 850 Nm at 2,500–3,500 rpm |
| US curb mass | 6,746 lb / approximately 3,060 kg | 5,842 lb / approximately 2,650 kg |
| EU running-order mass | 6,801 lb / 3,085 kg | 5,820 lb / 2,640 kg |

The power, torque and mass entries come from the linked US guides and [Mercedes electric technical table](https://media.mercedes-benz.com/press-kit/e3fb1e08-0558-4228-adb8-534c42940074/article/c39b603f-e8e9-4145-b521-39b394092fc1). The AMG dimensions and EU mass are cross-checked against the manufacturer-authored [July 2025 G 63 product sheet](https://mercedes-benzmodels.com/collateral/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/The-Mercedes-AMG-G-63-1.pdf). Regional trim, measurement definitions and equipment differ: do not mix US curb mass with EU running-order mass or automatically subtract a nominal driver from either figure. The regional AMG sheet lists 1.653 m for both tracks; this module's parent should retain a documented single-market mechanical selection.

**EV dimension discrepancy:** the US press table labels 182 inches as including the Design Box, but the European technical table gives 4,624 mm without clarifying the rear attachment. The [2025 Canadian owner PDF, printed page 344](https://www.mercedes-benz.ca/content/dam/mb-nafta/ca/owners/operators-manual/2025/MY2025%20Electric%20G%20Class%20SUV%20465%20584%2083%2000%20EN_A.pdf) repeats 4,624 mm and explicitly has editorially unavailable values elsewhere. The [later Mercedes owner dimension table](https://www.mercedes-benz.ca/en/owners/manuals/g-class-suv-2026-05-e465-mbux/vehicle-data/vehicle-dimensions) distinguishes 4,624 mm without rear attachment from 4,728 mm with Design Box and 4,863 mm with spare. A [2025 manufacturer Edition One product sheet](https://www.mercedes-benz.com.vn/content/dam/vietnam/passengercars/price-brochure/suv/G%20580%20EQ%20Product%20Sheet.pdf) independently shows approximately 4,730 mm with box. The original geometry therefore uses a 4.624 m base envelope plus a shallow box, targeting approximately 4.728 m overall; exact trim-dependent outermost badge/trim points remain reconstructed. That is a reasoned modeling choice, not a corrected official US certification figure.

## Drivetrain and suspension integration

The electric model must carry `electric: true`, `electricMotors: 4` and the **116 kWh usable** battery figure from the archived 2025 launch. Four independent motor commands provide virtual differential control. Treating this as two mechanically locked axles would misrepresent the vehicle. Low range is **2:1**; each wheel has an individual shiftable transmission. Do not claim G-TURN or G-STEERING unless the native physics actually implements the independent wheel commands and operating constraints. [Mercedes electric drivetrain explanation](https://www.mercedes-benz.com.au/passengercars/brand/mercedes-me-magazine/innovation/articles/g-class-electric-technology-off-road-vehicles.html), [US launch drivetrain and low-range section](https://media.mbusa.com/news/the-gelandewagen-electrified).

The G 63 uses permanent 40:60 front/rear AWD and selectable center, rear and front mechanical locks. Its nine forward ratios are **5.354, 3.243, 2.252, 1.636, 1.211, 1.000, 0.865, 0.717, 0.601**, with **4.798 reverse** and **2.93:1 low range**. The 48 V starter-generator's additional 20 hp / 15 kW and 148 lb-ft / 200 Nm are transient assistance, not a permanent addition to peak V8 torque. Standard adaptive damping and optional AMG Active Ride Control are distinct equipment choices; modeling ordinary coils/dampers does not establish a simulation of its hydraulic roll-control system. [Mercedes 2025 launch technical and transmission data](https://media.mbusa.com/news/the-gelandewagen-off-road-unlimited-established-in-1979), [AMG manufacturer description](https://www.mercedes-amg.com/en/g-class-suv).

No verified primary source located here provides exact stock final-drive reduction, center of gravity, static axle weights, wheel rates, damping coefficients or usable wheel travel. Initial **simulation estimates**, to be calibrated in native handling tests, are:

| Estimated simulator input | G 580 | G 63 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CG height above nominal contact plane | 28.7 in / 0.73 m | 31.5 in / 0.80 m |
| Static front mass fraction | 0.50 | 0.54 |
| Suspension travel | 9.4 in / 0.24 m | 8.7 in / 0.22 m |
| Rest-length parameter | 14.2 in / 0.36 m | 13.4 in / 0.34 m |
| Effective per-wheel spring rate | approximately 371 lb/in / 65,000 N/m | approximately 354 lb/in / 62,000 N/m |
| Effective per-wheel damping | 7,200 N·s/m | 6,500 N·s/m |
| Unverified high/final reduction | approximately 12.4 motor-to-wheel, then ×2 low | approximately 3.45 axle reduction |

These estimates are deliberately separate from `GCLASS_REFERENCE_SPECS`' published values. Preserve the true heavy EV mass; tune support and body collision rather than lightening it to hide suspension compression. Check full bump and rebound, low-speed rollback/braking, stock tire support and frame/battery clearance. Do not claim arbitrary estimated reductions as Mercedes gear ratios. The G 63's mechanical locks and the electric model's virtual control should retain different capability metadata even if a simulator approximation shares some control UI.

## Photo inspection and distinguishing geometry

The inspected source set includes the manufacturer's [AMG front view, profile, wheel, spare-cover, door-handle and three-quarter photographs](https://www.mercedes-amg.com/en/g-class-suv), the launch photographs on the [G 63 media page](https://media.mbusa.com/news/the-gelandewagen-off-road-unlimited-established-in-1979), the [G 580 launch imagery](https://media.mbusa.com/news/the-gelandewagen-electrified), the [electric retail exterior/Design Box images](https://www.mercedes-benz.co.in/passengercars/models/suv/g-class-electric/overview.html), the 2025 electric product-sheet front/rear/quarter tiles and dimension diagram, and the owner-manual side diagram. Roof, bonnet and greenhouse details are assessed in elevated front and rear quarters rather than assuming an unavailable true orthographic photograph. Some direct image downloads returned HTTP 403; those are not represented as locally saved photographs. Publicly displayed reference views were inspected through the source pages/PDF views. The local research directory identifies what was actually saved.

The shared shape retains the nearly straight roof and upright rear door, narrow straight A/B/C/D pillars, three separate side panes per side, windscreen rake, rain gutters, external door hinges, push-button pull handles, separate wing-top indicator pods, shallow bonnet crown and pressed ridges, raised side protective molding, formed projecting fenders, running-board tread and low horizontal rear lamps. The glass occupies actual holes in the body sheet. Separate aperture returns, seals and lens internals prevent the flat opaque window stickers that would obscure a cabin.

The **G 580** selects the optional factory **Black Panel grille with LED outline**, standard-style rectangular Design Box and 20-inch AMG ten-spoke aero-oriented wheels. The rear box has a closed lid, rim, latch and emblem. There are no exhaust tips and no exposed spare tire. A closed rear-quarter charging flap and flat battery shield distinguish it underneath. Selection of that optional grille is recorded in model metadata; this is not presented as every base-market G 580's standard grille.

The **G 63** has a deep vertical-bar AMG grille, larger outer lower bumper inlets with vertical guides, wider projecting wheel-arch treatment, five-twin-spoke 20-inch AMG wheels, V8 BITURBO geometry lettering, four side-exit exhaust tips ahead of the rear wheels and a circular spare enclosure with metal ring. Rear reflectors are static materials; only the two rear lamps and high-mounted center emitter use the dynamic brake role. The two white reverse sections use a separate reverse role. The visible five-seat cabin has paired wide instrument/media displays, circular vents and a passenger grab handle, while remaining a simplified original reconstruction.

## API and bounded verification

`gClassView(spec)` accepts `bodyStyle: 'g580'` or `'g63'` and returns the established `VehicleView` interface. `buildGClass(view, spec)` can build into an empty body. `refineGClass(view, spec)` replaces the factory tires/rims while preserving `root`, `tireShape`, `tireSpin` and `rimSpin`, and returns `{tire, rim}` from wheel zero for the existing separate OBJ pipeline. `gClassRim(spec)` is also exported. Use `refineGClass` instead of the older generic `refineVehicle`; skip generic `bodyRefinement`. The established `vehicleFinishes` may still assign automotive roles. Body placement follows the existing datum: `Y = -cgHeight`, `Z = (0.5 - frontWeightFraction) × wheelbase`. No mechanical axle hardpoint is moved by the body builder.

The source-only check exercises both complete variants, nine glazing sight lines each, finite positions, unit normals, variant hardware, dynamic lamp separation and split wheel interfaces. It does **not** launch Unreal, export source assets, import, cook, package or run a native game. After the rear-header correction, the EV body contains **248,241 vertices / 356,596 triangles**, and the AMG body **266,752 vertices / 376,180 triangles**. The source contains many small meshes, but the established OBJ/static-mesh import combines them by material; these source mesh counts are not a measured native draw-call count. No texture allocation, production LOD or frame-time improvement is claimed from polygon count alone.

Diagnostic native rear views exposed a small open joint between the rear sheet and roof lip on both variants. Four bounded painted surfaces now form a real header, top web and end returns beneath the existing lip. They add 801 vertices per body while retaining every pre-existing mesh's geometry, normals, UVs and pose, and the exact original overall roof/length/width bounds. A separate private source check passed **89 checks**, including 15 independent former-gap rays per variant and unchanged first-hit glazing on all nine window probes per variant. This header diagnostic is separate from the public **88 expedition source checks**; it does not add to that count or establish final native visual acceptance. The fresh cooked rear views still require inspection.

Before acceptance, the parent integration needs front/side/rear/two-quarter/elevated native captures for both vehicles; comparison to the linked exact-trim sources; steering, compression, lamp and tire checks; coachwork support probes; stock and upgraded handling; and a fresh complete import/cook. The reconstructed panel stations, overhang distribution, grille optics, mirror internals, interiors and wheel surfacing remain approximations. There are no scanned body panels, baked manufacturing detail, production UV unwraps, complete OEM interiors or bespoke production LODs in this module. A recognizable, more detailed original vehicle is the intended result; manufacturer-level surface accuracy and Forza production quality remain outside the demonstrated evidence.
