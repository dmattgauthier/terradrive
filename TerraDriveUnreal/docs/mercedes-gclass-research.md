# Mercedes G 580 and AMG G 63: pass 15 proportions and original geometry

Research checked October 28, 2026. The target is the **2025 modern G 580 with EQ Technology** and **2025 Mercedes-AMG G 63**, rather than the older W463, a Brabus conversion, a G 63 4×4² or the earlier Concept EQG. This document describes source work and integration recommendations. It is not native-game acceptance, an OEM CAD reconstruction or a claim of Forza-level fidelity.

## Pass 15 measured corrections

The largest error was the vertical placement of the front body and windows. Comparing the saved native side captures with the manufacturer-authored [March 2025 G-Class brochure, printed page 84](https://6656052.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/6656052/2025%20Brochures/Mercedes-Benz-The-New-G-Class-SUV-March.pdf) showed that a correctly sized wheelbase was being obscured by an excessively tall bonnet/front wing and nearly equal side-window widths. The diagram explicitly labels **49.1 inches / 1,246 mm above the front axle**, **34.9 inches / 887 mm front overhang**, **33.4 inches / 849 mm rear bumper overhang**, and the **113.8-inch / 2,890 mm wheelbase**. The diagram's top/side/front/rear views and wheel close-ups were rendered and actually inspected, alongside the 2025 Singapore/Philippines G 580 and G 63 product-sheet front-quarter images, the manufacturer's front/wheel detail images, and the blue G 580 [press-side photograph reproduced by CarExpert](https://www.carexpert.com.au/car-news/2025-mercedes-benz-g580-price-and-specs-g-wagen-ev-priced-for-australia). The latter is visual cross-check evidence, not the technical specification authority.

The final original geometry retains the wheelbase and roof height while lowering the bonnet, light units, cowl, door molding and glass together. The G 63 bonnet now peaks at approximately **50.1 inches / 1.272 m**, with its front-axle section checked separately against the 49.1-inch diagram datum. The EV bonnet remains approximately **1.2 inches higher**. The bumper ends are redistributed by **3.27 inches / 83 mm** relative to the axles, with the rear door and attached box/spare moved forward coherently; total length is retained. Side glass is lower, the rear-door pane is appreciably narrower than the front pane, and the rear-quarter pane is intermediate in width. Final glass-sheet widths are approximately **31.3 / 26.9 / 28.7 inches** (front/rear/quarter), after rubber-seal insets and the true corner fillets. The front pane's rounded lower A-pillar corner reduces its extreme width without moving the B/C pillars or pane-height stations. These panel positions are estimates measured from a rasterized manufacturer drawing, not OEM engineering coordinates.

The bonnet has a compound crown above a separate clamshell return, seam and flatter front-wing ledge. The outer wheel arches use a broad outward-facing painted face, upper return and closed dark wheelwell, rather than a single rising strip that reflects the ground. The side panels have drawn relief, and the bumper corners have actual side depth with outward-facing normals. The G 580 additionally has smoother raised bonnet returns, shaped A-pillar fairings, rear wheel-arch curtain forms and a fuller roof lip. Mercedes specifically identifies its raised hood, A-pillar trim, roof spoiler and rear-arch air curtains as EV distinctions. [Manufacturer launch description](https://media.mbusa.com/news/the-gelandewagen-electrified), [Mercedes Australia exterior explanation](https://www.mercedes-benz.com.au/passengercars/brand/mercedes-me-magazine/performance/articles/first-drive-electric-g-class-g580-eq-technology.html).

All twenty initial pass-15 native G-Class views were inspected against the earlier native sides/quarters and manufacturer reference. That comparison rejected the first candidate's inflated front shoulders, downward-bending protective strip and proud upper fascia corners. The second source iteration replaced the broad quarter-ellipse with a distinct hood return and flatter wing ledge, preserved a level protective band across the nose, joined the fascia's top corners to the actual wing section, and corrected arch-face/wheelwell orientation. Independent inspection of all 90 second-candidate roster captures, including all 20 G-Class views at full resolution, confirmed those improvements but rejected the deep roof/header slab, almost square glazing corners and wavy longitudinal hood seam.

The third source iteration retains the corrected envelope, wheelbase, glass-height stations, arches, straight molding and rigid lamps. Its roof crown now rolls down to a shallow formed flange with a narrow dark gutter. The actual side-window holes, glass and seal contours use circular tangent fillets of approximately **3.1–3.3 inches**, with separate painted reveals; the windscreen and rear glazing receive corresponding corner treatment. The hood/wing joint is defined as a simple descending line in final contact space, then inverse-mapped into the authoring space, preventing the common proportion fit from introducing an S-shaped wave. The raised EV bonnet and AMG power ribs are retained. Neutral gray wing-indicator lenses replace the solid orange blocks. The front sheet is triangulated directly in its final X/Y plane around real circular lamp openings, with only numerically degenerate projected-area slivers removed; a separate guard rejects any substantive reversed triangle. The final complete import/cook and fresh third-candidate native captures were reviewed after these repairs. The earlier captures remain rejected diagnostic evidence.

A subsequent independent check of the same brochure's front drawing found that preserving circular lamps alone was insufficient: their diameter was approximately **11.9 inches**, substantially larger than the reference. In a high-resolution private crop, the published 1,979 mm vehicle height spans 898 pixels; the lamp diameter spans about 96–100 pixels, the centers are 576 pixels apart, and their height above the ground is approximately 412–413 pixels. The complete lens, bezel, seal and optical assembly is now uniformly sized to **8.58 inches / 0.218 m**, placed **50.0 inches / 1.270 m apart**, and centered **35.8 inches / 0.909 m above the contact plane**. Matching circular openings retain a small assembly clearance. The grille opening is correspondingly narrowed to **34.5 inches / 0.876 m**, with its backing and bars kept inside it. These are estimates measured from the manufacturer's raster drawing, with line-thickness tolerance; Mercedes does not publish these lamp dimensions as engineering specifications. The G 63 leading hood center remains at approximately **42.8 inches / 1.087 m**, close to the drawing's **42.3 inches / 1.075 m** estimate, so this correction does not lower or stretch the nose. The source body's negative CG offset cancels the native stock garage's positive CG placement; these measurements are from the tire contact plane.

The G 63 now selects the factory **R20 optional 22-inch forged multi-spoke wheel**, shown on brochure page 66, with **295/40R22** road-performance tires. The original reconstruction has ten split pairs, open gaps, a thin machined edge, barrel, centre cap and five bolts. This makes the rim-to-tire relationship visibly different from the EV's retained 20-inch wheels. Nominal G 63 tire radius is **15.65 inches / 0.3974 m**, width **11.61 inches / 0.295 m**; these dimensions are also the simulator's stock tire values. This is the brochure R20 multi-spoke option, not the separate RG4 cross-spoke design shown on the [current manufacturer AMG page](https://www.mercedes-benz.com.sg/passengercars/models/suv/g-class/amg.html).

The proportion fit uses monotonic sections and inverse-transpose normal transforms; it does not flatten the whole vehicle or rescale the axle spacing. Round lamps, star badges, the complete covered spare, mirrors, door handles and the EV Design Box move as rigid detail groups, retaining their local shapes. The front sheet is constructed in final reference space so its lamp apertures remain circular. The standalone validator checks the hood height, glass hierarchy/height and actual radiused opening corners, final-space seam straightness, roof roll/flange depth, flat wing ledges and outward returns, outward painted arches and inward dark wheelwells, level molding, fascia shading normals and actual triangle winding, tire selection, drawing-derived lamp size/spacing/height, circular spare dimensions, 64 rays around each variant's circular openings plus four across its separate grille opening, and positive vertical Jacobians throughout the fitted envelope. **67 focused source checks** pass in `Tools/validate-gclass-shape.mjs`, alongside the separate 88 expedition checks. Those assertions are source evidence. The new cooked native front, side, rear and quarter captures were separately inspected for the final [native acceptance](validation-native.md). Private reference files are under `Saved/GClassRefinement15`; they are excluded from publication.

The original model authoring module is [mercedes-gclass.mjs](../Tools/mercedes-gclass.mjs). Reference photographs, downloaded research pages and private source checks belong under `Saved/Expedition14References/GClass`; they are inspection material and are excluded from public asset publication. The body uses authored vertices, curves and primitives. No photograph is a vehicle texture and no external vehicle mesh is included.

## Verified specification choices

Both vehicles use a **113.8-inch wheelbase**. The G 580 retains **275/50R20**, with a calculated nominal radius of **15.41 inches**, width **10.83 inches** and diameter **30.83 inches**. The G 63 selects the optional 22-inch fitment documented above. Tire dimensions are nominal sidewall arithmetic, not measured loaded radii. The electric model has four independently controlled motors and a two-speed transmission per motor; the motors are mounted to the frame, rather than being hub motors. Its rear suspension is De-Dion, while the G 63 has a driven solid rear axle. Both have independent double-wishbone front suspension. [Mercedes-Benz USA 2025 G 580 guide](https://media.mbusa.com/news/2025-mercedes-benz-g-580-with-eq-technology-quick-reference-guide), [2025 G 63 guide](https://media.mbusa.com/news/2025-mercedes-amg-g-63-quick-reference-guide), [electric launch technical data](https://media.mbusa.com/news/the-gelandewagen-electrified).

| --- | --- | --- |
| Rated propulsion | 579 hp / 432 kW; 859 lb-ft / 1,164 Nm aggregate | 577 hp / 430 kW at 6,000 rpm; 627 lb-ft / 850 Nm at 2,500–3,500 rpm |
| US curb mass | 6,746 lb / approximately 3,060 kg | 5,842 lb / approximately 2,650 kg |
| EU running-order mass | 6,801 lb / 3,085 kg | 5,820 lb / 2,640 kg |
| Stock front/rear tracks selected | 64.5 / 64.4 in; 1.638 / 1.637 m | 65.0 / 65.2 in; approximately 1.651 / 1.656 m |
| Body width excluding mirrors | 76.0 in / 1.931 m reference envelope | 78.1 in / 1.984 m |
| Width including mirrors | 86.1 in / 2.187 m | 86.1 in / 2.187 m |
| Reference roof height | 78.2 in / 1.986 m | 77.9 in / 1.979 m |
| Selected rear attachment | Rectangular Design Box, no external tire | Circular covered spare |
| Reference overall length | Approximately 186.1 in / 4.728 m with Design Box; see discrepancy below | Approximately 191.9 in / 4.873 m with spare, excluding brush guard |

| Stock top speed | 112 mph, electronically limited | 137 mph, electronically limited; 149 mph is optional equipment |

The power, torque and mass entries come from the linked US guides and [Mercedes electric technical table](https://media.mercedes-benz.com/press-kit/e3fb1e08-0558-4228-adb8-534c42940074/article/c39b603f-e8e9-4145-b521-39b394092fc1). The AMG dimensions and EU mass are cross-checked against the manufacturer-authored [July 2025 G 63 product sheet](https://mercedes-benzmodels.com/collateral/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/The-Mercedes-AMG-G-63-1.pdf). Regional trim, measurement definitions and equipment differ: do not mix US curb mass with EU running-order mass or automatically subtract a nominal driver from either figure. The regional AMG sheet lists 1.653 m for both tracks; this module's parent should retain a documented single-market mechanical selection.

**EV dimension discrepancy:** the US press table labels 182 inches as including the Design Box, but the European technical table gives 4,624 mm without clarifying the rear attachment. The [2025 Canadian owner PDF, printed page 344](https://www.mercedes-benz.ca/content/dam/mb-nafta/ca/owners/operators-manual/2025/MY2025%20Electric%20G%20Class%20SUV%20465%20584%2083%2000%20EN_A.pdf) repeats 4,624 mm and explicitly has editorially unavailable values elsewhere. The [later Mercedes owner dimension table](https://www.mercedes-benz.ca/en/owners/manuals/g-class-suv-2026-05-e465-mbux/vehicle-data/vehicle-dimensions) distinguishes 4,624 mm without rear attachment from 4,728 mm with Design Box and 4,863 mm with spare. A [2025 manufacturer Edition One product sheet](https://www.mercedes-benz.com.vn/content/dam/vietnam/passengercars/price-brochure/suv/G%20580%20EQ%20Product%20Sheet.pdf) independently shows approximately 4,730 mm with box. The original geometry therefore uses a 4.624 m base envelope plus a shallow box, targeting approximately 4.728 m overall; exact trim-dependent outermost badge/trim points remain reconstructed. That is a reasoned modeling choice, not a corrected official US certification figure.

## Drivetrain and suspension integration

The electric model must carry `electric: true`, `electricMotors: 4` and the **116 kWh usable** battery figure from the archived 2025 launch. Four independent motor commands provide virtual differential control. Treating this as two mechanically locked axles would misrepresent the vehicle. Low range is **2:1**; each wheel has an individual shiftable transmission. Do not claim G-TURN or G-STEERING unless the native physics actually implements the independent wheel commands and operating constraints. [Mercedes electric drivetrain explanation](https://www.mercedes-benz.com.au/passengercars/brand/mercedes-me-magazine/innovation/articles/g-class-electric-technology-off-road-vehicles.html), [US launch drivetrain and low-range section](https://media.mbusa.com/news/the-gelandewagen-electrified).

The G 63 uses permanent 40:60 front/rear AWD and selectable center, rear and front mechanical locks. Its nine forward ratios are **5.354, 3.243, 2.252, 1.636, 1.211, 1.000, 0.865, 0.717, 0.601**, with **4.798 reverse** and **2.93:1 low range**. The 48 V starter-generator's additional 20 hp / 15 kW and 148 lb-ft / 200 Nm are transient assistance, not a permanent addition to peak V8 torque. Standard adaptive damping and optional AMG Active Ride Control are distinct equipment choices; modeling ordinary coils/dampers does not establish a simulation of its hydraulic roll-control system. [Mercedes 2025 launch technical and transmission data](https://media.mbusa.com/news/the-gelandewagen-off-road-unlimited-established-in-1979), [AMG manufacturer description](https://www.mercedes-amg.com/en/g-class-suv).

No verified primary source located here provides exact stock final-drive reduction, center of gravity, static axle weights, wheel rates, damping coefficients or usable wheel travel. Initial **simulation estimates**, to be calibrated in native handling tests, are:

| Estimated simulator input | G 580 | G 63 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CG height above nominal contact plane | 28.7 in / 0.73 m | 31.5 in / 0.80 m |
| Static front mass fraction | 0.50 | 0.54 |
| Suspension travel | 9.4 in / 0.24 m | 8.7 in / 0.22 m |
| Rest-length parameter | 14.2 in / 0.36 m | 13.4 in / 0.34 m |
| Effective per-wheel spring rate | approximately 371 lb/in / 65,000 N/m | approximately 354 lb/in / 62,000 N/m |
| Effective per-wheel damping | 7,200 N·s/m | 6,500 N·s/m |
| Unverified high/final reduction | approximately 12.4 motor-to-wheel, then ×2 low | approximately 3.45 axle reduction |

These estimates are deliberately separate from `GCLASS_REFERENCE_SPECS`' published values. Preserve the true heavy EV mass; tune support and body collision rather than lightening it to hide suspension compression. Check full bump and rebound, low-speed rollback/braking, stock tire support and frame/battery clearance. Do not claim arbitrary estimated reductions as Mercedes gear ratios. The G 63's mechanical locks and the electric model's virtual control should retain different capability metadata even if a simulator approximation shares some control UI.

## Photo inspection and distinguishing geometry

The inspected source set includes the manufacturer's [AMG front view, profile, wheel, spare-cover, door-handle and three-quarter photographs](https://www.mercedes-amg.com/en/g-class-suv), the launch photographs on the [G 63 media page](https://media.mbusa.com/news/the-gelandewagen-off-road-unlimited-established-in-1979), the [G 580 launch imagery](https://media.mbusa.com/news/the-gelandewagen-electrified), the [electric retail exterior/Design Box images](https://www.mercedes-benz.co.in/passengercars/models/suv/g-class-electric/overview.html), the 2025 electric product-sheet front/rear/quarter tiles and dimension diagram, and the owner-manual side diagram. Roof, bonnet and greenhouse details are assessed in elevated front and rear quarters rather than assuming an unavailable true orthographic photograph. Some direct image downloads returned HTTP 403; those are not represented as locally saved photographs. Publicly displayed reference views were inspected through the source pages/PDF views. The local research directory identifies what was actually saved.

The shared shape retains the nearly straight roof and upright rear door, narrow straight A/B/C/D pillars, three separate side panes per side, windscreen rake, rain gutters, external door hinges, push-button pull handles, separate wing-top indicator pods, shallow bonnet crown and pressed ridges, raised side protective molding, formed projecting fenders, running-board tread and low horizontal rear lamps. The glass occupies actual holes in the body sheet. Separate aperture returns, seals and lens internals prevent the flat opaque window stickers that would obscure a cabin.

The **G 580** selects the optional factory **Black Panel grille with LED outline**, standard-style rectangular Design Box and 20-inch AMG ten-spoke aero-oriented wheels. The rear box has a closed lid, rim, latch and emblem. There are no exhaust tips and no exposed spare tire. A closed rear-quarter charging flap and flat battery shield distinguish it underneath. Selection of that optional grille is recorded in model metadata; this is not presented as every base-market G 580's standard grille.

The **G 63** has a deep vertical-bar AMG grille, larger outer lower bumper inlets with vertical guides, wider projecting wheel-arch treatment, the selected 22-inch forged multi-spoke AMG wheels, V8 BITURBO geometry lettering, four side-exit exhaust tips ahead of the rear wheels and a circular spare enclosure with metal ring. Rear reflectors are static materials; only the two rear lamps and high-mounted center emitter use the dynamic brake role. The two white reverse sections use a separate reverse role. The visible five-seat cabin has paired wide instrument/media displays, circular vents and a passenger grab handle, while remaining a simplified original reconstruction.

## API and bounded verification

`gClassView(spec)` accepts `bodyStyle: 'g580'` or `'g63'` and returns the established `VehicleView` interface. `buildGClass(view, spec)` can build into an empty body. `refineGClass(view, spec)` replaces the factory tires/rims while preserving `root`, `tireShape`, `tireSpin` and `rimSpin`, and returns `{tire, rim}` from wheel zero for the existing separate OBJ pipeline. `gClassRim(spec)` is also exported. Use `refineGClass` instead of the older generic `refineVehicle`; skip generic `bodyRefinement`. The established `vehicleFinishes` may still assign automotive roles. Body placement follows the existing datum: `Y = -cgHeight`, `Z = (0.5 - frontWeightFraction) × wheelbase`. No mechanical axle hardpoint is moved by the body builder.

The source-only check exercises both complete variants, nine glazing sight lines each, finite positions, unit normals, variant hardware, dynamic lamp separation and split wheel interfaces. It does **not** launch Unreal, export source assets, import, cook, package or run a native game. The third pass-15 candidate with the independently corrected lamps contains **276,514 vertices / 394,336 triangles** in the EV body and **292,713 vertices / 410,064 triangles** in the AMG body. The source contains many small meshes, but the established OBJ/static-mesh import combines them by material; these source mesh counts are not a measured native draw-call count. No texture allocation, production LOD or frame-time improvement is claimed from polygon count alone.

Pass 14 diagnostic rear views exposed a small open joint between the rear sheet and roof lip. Its four bounded painted header/top-web/end-return surfaces remain in pass 15 and move with the surrounding coachwork. The earlier 89-check private header report describes the historical pass 14 fix; it is not a claim that pass 15 retained unchanged vertex positions. Current geometry and native inspection are reported separately.

The final pass-15 integration completed a fresh full import/cook and actual review of front/side/rear/quarter/elevated native captures for both vehicles against the linked sources, together with stock/upgraded handling, lamp/tire, ground-support and rollover checks. Exact current acceptance is recorded in [native validation](validation-native.md). The reconstructed panel stations, overhang distribution, grille optics, mirror internals, interiors and wheel surfacing remain approximations. There are no scanned body panels, baked manufacturing detail, production UV unwraps, complete OEM interiors or bespoke production LODs in this module. A recognizable, more detailed original vehicle is the intended result; manufacturer-level surface accuracy and Forza production quality remain outside the demonstrated evidence.
