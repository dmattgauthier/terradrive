# Vehicle art: moving beyond procedural approximations

Research and proposed production plan: October 22, 2026. This is a quality plan, not a claim that the existing six vehicles meet it. The browser version remains preserved.

## What is limiting the current result

The native vehicles are assembled from scripted surfaces, primitives and reference dimensions. They are useful driving prototypes. Correct overall dimensions and working lamps do not establish accurate panel curvature, glass openings, wheel arches, lamp optics, wheel designs or interiors. The HUMMER shown in the reported ground-contact screenshot still has flat body transitions, simplified glazing and generic detail. More polygons in those same shapes will not correct the silhouette.

Turn 10 describes licensing, sourcing a pristine vehicle, 3D scanning and modeling before game integration in its [official production explanation](https://forza.net/news/community-hubs-update-3). A [Forza vehicle artist's first-hand portfolio](https://stryking.artstation.com/projects/qeWldz) describes manufacturer-reference requirements, exterior and interior materials, UVs, damage maps and multiple LODs. These are evidence for the production process; no undocumented Forza polygon budget or development-time estimate is assumed here.

## Recommended first vehicle: original Urus Performante

Finish one vehicle to an agreed visual standard before repeating the process across the garage. Keep the original V8 Performante, rather than mixing details from the newer SE. Use [Lamborghini's original model reference](https://www.lamborghini.com/en-en/history/urus-performante) and its [original brochure](https://www.lamborghini.com/original/DAM/lamborghini/facelift_2019/model_detail/gateway_urus/performante/2022/banner/pdf/2023/URUS%20PERFORMANCE_DIGITAL_BROCHURE_WCAG_EN.pdf). Retain the intended 202.24-inch length, 79.76-inch body width, 63.70-inch height and 118.34-inch wheelbase. Reference photographs are inspection material, not texture assets to redistribute.

1. **Obtain or author a strong source mesh.** The fastest likely route is a legitimate, detailed model of the exact trim, then game conversion. The alternative is manual hard-surface modeling in a DCC application using orthographic references and measured hardpoints; it requires substantial vehicle-art work. A generic Urus, a render-only shell, or an attractive store thumbnail does not meet acceptance.
2. **Approve the silhouette before detail.** Compare front, side, rear and three-quarter views against matched reference camera angles. Check wheel centers, track, tire diameter, overhangs, roof profile, windscreen rake, hood crown, beltline, rear-quarter taper and bumper openings. Use reflection strips to expose waviness and pinching in curved panels. Do not compensate for wrong proportions with lighting.
3. **Author real components.** Separate paint panels, seals and panel gaps, inner wheel wells, underbody, brake discs and calipers, steering wheels, tires, lenses and lamp internals. Include the visible cabin, seat shapes, dash and door cards. On the Performante, verify the hood treatment, bumper/intakes, rear diffuser, exhausts, spoiler and correct factory wheel design.
4. **Prepare game topology and UVs.** Keep curvature where it affects silhouette and reflections; bake small detail from the high-resolution model. Supply consistent normals/tangents, UVs, normal/AO/roughness maps and sensible material slots. Inspect smoothing and seam artifacts in Unreal. Start with a measured target budget, then reduce cost based on profiling the packaged game on the RTX 4060 laptop; a polygon count alone is not a quality test.
5. **Build automotive materials.** Use separate clear-coated paint, plastic, rubber, metal, carbon, glass and lens materials. [Epic's Automotive Materials documentation](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/automotive-materials-pack-in-unreal-engine) describes paint flakes, orange peel, clear coat and automotive surface types. Its [Fab pack](https://www.fab.com/listings/5dd132fe-ee32-4e8c-9cd3-7496547dfb29) was listed free when checked. The example car meshes are explicitly not included. Materials help an accurate mesh; they do not create one.
6. **Integrate art without changing mechanical hardpoints.** Retain the native tire/soil solver, suspension travel, lamp controls and upgrade stages. Give tires the proper axle pivots and keep calipers stationary. Use simple, intentional chassis collision and separate wheel support. Detailed render meshes must not turn into an expensive or snag-prone vehicle collider. The [UE FBX pipeline](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/fbx-static-mesh-pipeline-in-unreal-engine) supports multiple UV sets, material slots, custom collision and LODs.
7. **Validate in ordinary gameplay.** Compare close garage views, cloudy/daylight/driving views, braking/reverse lamps, full steering and suspension compression, dirt accumulation, upgrades and underside views. Profile frame time, memory, draw calls, texture streaming and LOD transitions with all six vehicle choices available. An offline beauty render is insufficient.
8. **Repeat only after the first vehicle passes.** Apply the same reference and material standard to the HUMMER, Wrangler, Bronco, Civic and Sprinter, including their exact model year/body configuration. Avoid another round of superficial changes across all six before one body reaches the quality bar.

## Concrete sourcing findings

| [Render Rides Urus on Fab](https://www.fab.com/listings/f9b73984-d8c7-42e9-89f7-75cc85b29714) | Publisher describes a rigged Urus and lists 500,605 polygons with FBX/OBJ/Blender. | Not demonstrated to be the original Performante. Price and license tier were not exposed by the page read. Do not substitute it merely because it is called Urus. |
| Epic Automotive Materials | Official free Fab listing and UE documentation. | Good material reference and possible integration source; separate from acquiring the correct vehicle mesh. |

No paid vehicle asset was purchased or imported during this investigation. No freely downloadable exact-trim Urus was verified to satisfy both this visual standard and the project's distribution requirements. Store claims are not independently verified mesh quality.

The public GitHub repository needs a deliberate asset boundary. A license allowing a cooked game does not automatically allow redistribution of the editable source model. For example, [TurboSquid's own licensing guidance](https://blog.turbosquid.com/royalty-free-license/) distinguishes embedded game use from open-format redistribution. Check the actual chosen asset's terms; use an external local asset location plus an import recipe if raw source sharing is not allowed. Manufacturer branding rights are separate from buying a mesh and need review before commercial distribution.

## Review gate for claiming visual accuracy

Record six matched reference/render views, a reflection-strip turntable and close-ups of lamps, wheels, arches and cabin. Reject visibly wrong roofline, glazing, track, arches or trim before polishing textures. Archive packaged-game captures and performance observations. Automated mesh-load checks establish technical integration only; visual approval must explicitly compare the rendered car to the real vehicle.
