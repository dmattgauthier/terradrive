# Winter round: vehicle surfaces and factory wheels

This native-only round improves the six existing authored vehicles. The Urus remains the original V8 Performante, rather than the newer SE variant. The preserved browser source and mechanical wheel hardpoints are unchanged. These are reference-built reconstructions, not manufacturer CAD or production racing-game assets.

The Urus bonnet now has two actual cooling apertures, rolled carbon lips, dark inset floors and individually recessed louvers. The former black patches sat on an intact painted bonnet. A continuous intake crossblade and angular radar housing give the front fascia more depth. At the rear, the hatch has a drawn waist, a genuine recessed number-plate well with painted returns, a hatch shut line and a separate handle/light area. The roof spoiler now has an underside and closed leading/trailing edges, rather than a single thin sheet. Body revision is 7.

The HUMMER's formed metal front skid was already present but was largely hidden behind the uncut black bumper. The bumper now has a center cutout around that separate panel, with formed edge details and fasteners. The Wrangler's door skins now include broad shallow pressings and handle cups. The Bronco has a narrow, body-following left rear fuel-flap reveal. Civic and Sprinter body shapes retain the preceding round's reconstruction; their changes here are in the wheel/tire assemblies.

All six use new native tire carcasses and beveled tread geometry, with separate all-terrain, all-season, highway-commercial, mud-terrain and road-performance patterns. The Urus now selects the supported road-performance pattern. The factory mechanical diameter, width and bead fit still control the generated tire. Jeep, Bronco and HUMMER spares now use the same factory tire and rim construction as their driving wheels. The standard vehicles have plain brake friction rings; drilled carbon-ceramic detail is specific to the Urus. Native wheel/fidelity revision is 3. These are static authored assets; the changes add no per-frame procedural tread generation.

Reference comparison uses the retained manufacturer brochure pages under `Saved/VehicleResearch`, especially the Performante front and rear three-quarter photographs and GMC MY24 front views. The exact configuration catalogue is in [vehicle-model-research.md](vehicle-model-research.md). Public references revisited in this round are the [Lamborghini original Performante page](https://www.lamborghini.com/en-en/history/urus-performante), [Lamborghini launch design description](https://www.lamborghini.com/en-en/news/lamborghini-urus-performante-for-bar-raisers-only), [Jeep MY24 release](https://www.media.stellantis.com/uk-en/jeep/press/jeep-reveals-new-2024-wrangler), [Ford MY24 brochure](https://www.ford.mx/content/dam/Ford/website-assets/latam/mx/nameplate/bronco/2024/pdf/ford-bronco-2024-catalogo-descargable.pdf), [Honda MY24 model page](https://www.hondainfocenter.com/2024/Civic-Sedan/), [Mercedes MY16 Crew brochure](https://assets.mbvans.com/Mercedes-Benz-Vans/Brochures/Mercedes-Benz-Sprinter-Crew-Van.pdf) and [GMC MY24 SUV brochure](https://www.gmc.com/content/dam/gmc/na/us/english/index/about/download-brochures/2024-models/GMTH23CT000-2024-HUMMER-EV-SUV-FO-031924.pdf). Current general model pages can redirect to later generations, so those are not substituted for the retained exact-year appearance references. No manufacturer photographs are shipped as game textures.

`node Tools/validate-models.mjs` checks finite assembled surfaces, normalized normals, tire envelopes and independent ray paths through the Urus glass, bonnet vent and plate recess. Its passing source report is [model-validation.json](model-validation.json). The first vent test exposed incorrect face winding, which was corrected before the passing source geometry check. `node Tools/validate-export.mjs` checks the final exported model revisions and upgrade bead fit after export. Cooked native rendering, gameplay and thermal evidence must come from the final build; see [native-validation.json](native-validation.json) for the accepted build, rather than treating source geometry checks as native visual verification.

Small fonts, material layering, curved panel precision, interiors, UV-authored details and optimized production LODs remain areas for further art work. This pass does not claim Forza-level accuracy or a complete remesh of every body.
