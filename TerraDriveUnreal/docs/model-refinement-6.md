# All-vehicle coachwork round 6

This round refines all six existing vehicles using native-only authoring modules. The preserved browser files are unchanged. These are photograph-based reconstructions, not manufacturer CAD or scanned production assets.

| Vehicle | Geometry changes |
|---|---|
| Wrangler Rubicon | Flatter molded fender shelves, denser curved lip sections, smoother upholstery, inset window flanges and dark inward-facing reveals. |
| Bronco Badlands | Flatter flare crowns, finer continuous lip sections, light factory-style grille lettering, dark marine-style cabin palette and glazing returns. |
| Civic Sport sedan | Lower bonnet crown, broader and shallower door stampings, more finely sampled pillar ribbons and inner pillar trim. Sport mirrors, lamp layout and wheel hardpoints retained. |
| Sprinter W906 Crew | Inset inner side liners around the actual window apertures, a cleaner offset along the windscreen-side pillar edge, denser stamped arch shoulders and smoother seat/headrest surfaces. |
| HUMMER EV SUV | Broader, less exaggerated haunch transitions and waist recess, swept mirror covers with a separate reflecting face, smoother seating and deeper window returns. |
| Urus Performante | Open windshield and hatch surrounds replace opaque backing sheets. Swept angular mirrors, lamp covers, closed seat shells, headrest supports and a left-hand-drive instrument/steering layout improve close inspection. |

The five non-Urus builders now use project-owned body, fender and cabin modules. The seat and headrest normals follow continuous surface derivatives instead of the distribution of triangles around a superellipsoid. Window apertures retain their existing footprints and add a physical reveal and inward-facing trim. These refinements preserve the original drivetrain, suspension, upgrade attachment and wheel hardpoints. Epic Automotive Materials remains the acquired finish dependency; its original parent assets are not changed or uploaded.

The first close-camera review led to another correction pass: shallower SUV door dishing, visible Wrangler hood lettering, removal of the Civic's duplicate straight equipment lip, corrected Civic/Sprinter rear badge placement and dark inner trim over the HUMMER upper-door/quarter and Urus cargo/belt surfaces. The final trim correction completes the SUV fender edge trace and removes the unsupported extra Urus rear badge. These were identified through packaged-game renders, reference comparison and source review.

Reference comparison uses the existing exact-year/trim source catalogue in [vehicle-model-research.md](vehicle-model-research.md) and the V8 Performante references in [urus-automotive.md](urus-automotive.md). This round also revisited the [Jeep MY24 release](https://www.media.stellantis.com/uk-en/jeep/press/jeep-reveals-new-2024-wrangler), [Ford MY24 brochure](https://www.ford.mx/content/dam/Ford/website-assets/latam/mx/nameplate/bronco/2024/pdf/ford-bronco-2024-catalogo-descargable.pdf), [Honda MY24 model page](https://www.hondainfocenter.com/2024/Civic-Sedan/), [Honda Sport photograph](https://hondanews.com/en-US/releases/release-a463299e9046a088b84018a7580565c4/photos/0), [Mercedes MY16 Crew brochure](https://assets.mbvans.com/Mercedes-Benz-Vans/Brochures/Mercedes-Benz-Sprinter-Crew-Van.pdf), [GMC MY24 SUV brochure](https://www.gmc.com/content/dam/gmc/na/us/english/index/about/download-brochures/2024-models/GMTH23CT000-2024-HUMMER-EV-SUV-FO-031924.pdf) and [Lamborghini Performante page](https://www.lamborghini.com/en-en/history/urus-performante). Regional photographs inform shapes; optional equipment and different trims are not automatically adopted.

Run `node Tools/validate-models.mjs` to check finite assembled surfaces, normalized shading and actual clear paths through the Urus front/rear panes. `node Tools/validate-export.mjs` checks the exported assets, stock wheel diameter and upgraded tire beads. Native gameplay and rendering evidence is recorded after the final cooked build in [native-validation.json](native-validation.json). Close-up mesh accuracy, UV-authored trim detail, optical simulation and production LOD work remain below the standard of a Forza vehicle asset.

`Tools/verify-model-art.ps1` runs the real packaged game with an isolated save, checks 42 material/render cases, requires all 36 fresh close-camera captures and checks the actual process exit. It supplies matching front, side, rear and three-quarter views of every stock vehicle without the garage UI covering the body. These captures require a human visual review; a render-presence check alone cannot establish vehicle accuracy.
