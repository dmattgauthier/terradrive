Refinement occurs during export; the damage renderer uses the prepared mesh on accepted impacts without a runtime subdivision step. This is topology preparation, not a frame-time or GPU-cost guarantee.

Its final 100 frames average 300.000 ms per frame (95th percentile 454.209 ms). These parallel/overlapping counters must not be added. Mean available RAM is 3583 MiB; the profile does not establish RAM alone, the soil bounds change or a particular collision/geometry subroutine as the cause.
