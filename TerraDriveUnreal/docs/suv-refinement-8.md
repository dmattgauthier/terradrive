# SUV body surfaces, cowl openings and glazing

This round changes the native Wrangler Rubicon, Bronco Badlands and HUMMER EV SUV generators, together with glazing on all six vehicles. It preserves the browser implementation, factory wheel hardpoints, mechanical tire dimensions and vehicle catalogue. These are still authored reconstructions from photographs and published dimensions, with estimated small-panel shapes rather than manufacturer CAD.

The previous native three-quarter captures showed very smooth, long bonnets with little distinction between the bonnet and the windscreen cowl. The Wrangler and Bronco bonnets now end ahead of a separate formed cowl. That cowl has a genuine aperture, recessed dark walls and floor, and individual ventilation vanes. The original internal painted closure is cut back beneath the opening too, so it cannot accidentally fill the recess. The bonnet hinge assemblies, washer jets and rear shut lines give the assembly more depth when viewed from above.

The Bronco's broad central bonnet plateau is pressed into its continuous painted skin. Its rolled return is different from the Wrangler's narrower, more pronounced outer bonnet shoulders. Both vehicles gain drawn wheel haunches in the painted panels behind the plastic flares. The Wrangler also has an inset circular fuel cap on the left rear quarter, two-leaf windscreen hinges, a thinner upper windscreen frame, jointed wiper arms with bowed blades, and a camera in the spare's center. Both SUV spare assemblies receive the center camera.

The HUMMER's twin bonnet crests taper toward the nose around a shallow center well. Its previous raised black cowl pieces are replaced by actual bonnet openings with rolled reveals, set-back floors and separate vanes. The molded arch trim now has a flatter face and a small rolled lip, reducing the inflated highlight around each opening. Its dedicated body version is `1.16.0`; the shared native construction and wheel schema versions remain compatible. `bodySurfaceRevision: 8` identifies the new surface round.

The HUMMER's formed front skid also had an inward-facing sheet normal. Front-ray inspection caught that orientation, which is now corrected without changing its physical shape. Satin metal parts use a separate, rougher instance of the acquired Epic metal parent rather than inheriting polished chrome roughness. Actual chrome and the other finish roles retain their existing assignments.

First-candidate native review exposed a real inherited hole beside the HUMMER's slanted rear pane, where the rear glass and side quarter stopped independently. Rearward rays at those frame positions could pass through the cabin instead of striking bodywork. Formed D-pillars now turn continuously from the side quarters into the black rear-glass reveals. Lower corner returns and a lower glass belt close the gap above the gate too. Independent probes on both sides require the first opaque hit to lie on the actual rear frame. The first package's screenshots remain historical and do not establish acceptance of this corrected mesh.

Front, side and privacy panes retain transparent geometry and the existing native view-dependent glass shader. Darker grey-green tint and revised opacity reduce the unusually clear display-case appearance in the preceding captures. Privacy glass remains visibly darker than the front panes; the interior remains inspectable through the front glass.

The reference comparison used the preceding packaged native front, side and three-quarter images against [GMC's MY24 HUMMER EV SUV brochure](https://www.gmc.com/content/dam/gmc/na/us/english/index/about/download-brochures/2024-models/GMTH23CT000-2024-HUMMER-EV-SUV-FO-031924.pdf), including its rendered cover and model overview, [Jeep's MY24 Wrangler introduction](https://www.media.stellantis.com/uk-en/jeep/press/jeep-reveals-new-2024-wrangler), and the official imagery on [Ford's MY24 Bronco page](https://www.ford.com/local/los-angeles/competitive-compare/suvs/bronco/2024/2024-ford-bronco/). Ford's photographs include two-door and accessory-equipped vehicles; only shared bonnet, lamp, cowl and flare characteristics informed the retained four-door Badlands reconstruction. Their accessory bumpers, roof racks and doors are not substituted for the selected factory trim. Research downloads stay under `Saved/VehicleResearch` and are excluded from source publication; no manufacturer photograph is used as a game texture.

The independent source audit casts downward rays into the real cowl and bonnet apertures and compares raised bonnet sections. For the Wrangler and Bronco, the first opaque surface between cowl vanes is the dark inset floor, rather than paint over the opening. The HUMMER's vent floor lies below the bonnet sheet. Source checks also inspect finite positions/normals and retain more than 99.98% normalized body normals across all three SUVs. These source checks establish geometry construction; the final cook, native rendering, tire/body clearance, gameplay and thermal evidence are recorded separately in [native-validation.json](native-validation.json).

The bodies still need production art work for CAD-level panel matching, UV-authored trim, interiors, optimized LODs and the finest lamp optics. The changes improve the existing models without claiming production racing-game fidelity.
