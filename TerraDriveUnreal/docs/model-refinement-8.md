# Vehicle coachwork refinement 8

This native-only round develops the six existing reference-built vehicles. The preserved browser implementation, original acquired material parents, mechanical axle positions and tire dimensions remain intact. Manufacturer photographs are comparison references; they are not republished as vehicle textures. The resulting models are authored reconstructions, with simplified interiors and small fittings, rather than manufacturer CAD or production racing-game assets.

The original V8 **Urus Performante** receives a more pronounced continuous shoulder and recessed lower-door section, drawn handle cups and curved black pull handles. Rounded glass apertures, a crowned carbon roof and bowed front/rear glazing replace the particularly planar appearance of the preceding greenhouse. The bonnet has stronger raised outer blades and an inner trough around its real cooling apertures. Its carbon and painted panels occupy separate surface regions with shared boundaries, rather than overlapping sheets. The angular front intake openings taper into deep returns. The rear spoiler is now a closed cambered airfoil with separate lower, edge and tip surfaces. The side and roof changes remain within the original vehicle envelope and do not move the axles. Its body revision is 8.

The **Civic Sport sedan** now has open molded hexagonal grille cells with short cell returns and a recessed backing, replacing square wire grids. Its bumper cheek undercuts and upper jaw are formed together with the actual lamp/grille openings. The existing pre-facelift sedan roofline, separate trunk, Sport mirrors, low-profile tires and real rear plate recess are retained. The **W906 Sprinter Crew Van** gains a continuous waist swage, fuller wheel haunches, pressed handle cups and more bowed cab glazing. Those sheet changes carry the trim and shut lines with them, so the surface does not depend on loose raised strips. Its established 144-inch wheelbase and calibrated front/rear overhangs remain.

The **Wrangler and Bronco** have open perforated cowls between the bonnet and windscreen, with inset floors, returns, vanes, hinges and washer jets. The Bronco's central bonnet plateau and both vehicles' curved wheel-haunch sheets are formed in the body. The **HUMMER EV SUV** has actual bonnet vent apertures and their recessed returns/vanes, a stronger twin-crest bonnet and flatter formed arch sections. This work remains specific to the existing selected trims; it does not silently substitute the latest model year or the hybrid Urus SE.

Epic Automotive Materials continue to supply the acquired car paint, carbon, plastic, metal, rubber and cabin finish families. Project-owned carbon instances now respect the authored dark source tint instead of replacing every tint with a much lighter 0.14 linear grey. The original parent assets remain unchanged. Visual acceptance must come from the freshly cooked native game, particularly for dark carbon under outdoor lighting.

Reference comparison revisits the [original Lamborghini Urus Performante](https://www.lamborghini.com/en-en/history/urus-performante), its [manufacturer digital brochure](https://www.lamborghini.com/original/DAM/lamborghini/facelift_2019/model_detail/gateway_urus/performante/2022/banner/pdf/2023/URUS%20PERFORMANCE_DIGITAL_BROCHURE_WCAG_EN.pdf) and [launch design description](https://www.lamborghini.com/en-en/news/lamborghini-urus-performante-for-bar-raisers-only). The retained brochure front and rear three-quarter photographs expose the roof crown, cooling apertures, carbon parts and rear spoiler more clearly than a general current-model page. The Civic comparison uses [Honda's exact 2024 model resource](https://www.hondainfocenter.com/2024/Civic-Sedan/) and [2024 US specifications and features](https://hondanews.com/en-US/releases/release-5003aaa39c009393f5d06d620f091c7d-2024-honda-civic-sedan-specifications-features); Honda's same-generation profile image supplies the side silhouette, rather than a later facelift. The Sprinter comparison uses the [Mercedes-Benz 2016 Crew Van brochure](https://assets.mbvans.com/Mercedes-Benz-Vans/Brochures/Mercedes-Benz-Sprinter-Crew-Van.pdf), including its front three-quarter photograph and low-roof dimension drawing. Additional retained exact-year references for the other three vehicles are in [vehicle-model-research.md](vehicle-model-research.md).

`node Tools/validate-models.mjs` checks finite surfaces and normal streams for all six vehicles, factory tire fit, real Urus bonnet and hatch recesses, unobstructed glazing, roof crown, spoiler thickness, the physical shoulder-to-waist section, an actual Civic mesh opening versus its neighboring web, and Sprinter swage relief. It also checks outward handle/skid faces, inward-facing duct and grille returns, bonnet material attribution and both HUMMER rear glazing corners. The source report is [model-validation.json](model-validation.json). `node Tools/validate-urus-panels.mjs` independently samples all four fascia apertures and checks for hidden painted bonnet geometry beneath the carbon region; its source report is [urus-panel-validation.json](urus-panel-validation.json). Source checks do not establish native visual quality or gameplay; fresh packaged evidence belongs in [native-validation.json](native-validation.json) after import, full cook and capture review.

The main remaining art limits are production surface precision, detailed interiors/lighting optics, UV-authored small trim, panel construction at very close range and optimized production LODs. This round does not claim Forza-level accuracy.
